Why a swarm with edge intelligence

Faster response

Analysis happens on or near the UAV, so a hazard is flagged in the field instead of waiting for a cloud round trip.

Less data to send

Only location, confidence and key measurements travel onward, not every raw image.

Works when links fail

Decentralised coordination lets UAVs keep monitoring and re-assign tasks without constant connectivity.
