About the lab

Autonomous environmental intelligence, where UAVs act as mobile, intelligent environmental sensors.

Aim

Develop and validate an AI-enabled autonomous UAV swarm with edge computing for real-time environmental monitoring, hazard detection, early warning and disaster-management support.

The problem we address

Fixed stations, manual surveys and satellites each leave gaps in resolution, response time, access and cost. Individual drones add reach but limit coverage and endurance, and streaming all imagery to the cloud creates latency.

Our research question: how can autonomous UAV swarms collaboratively sense, analyse and respond to fast-changing environmental conditions in real time, within communication, energy and operational limits?

Vision

An Autonomous Environmental Intelligence Network that moves monitoring from periodic observation to continuous awareness, and from centralised collection to distributed edge intelligence.

UAVs, edge computing, AI, swarm intelligence and digital twins are enabling technologies. Environmental intelligence stays the central objective.

Every UAV can

  • sense environmental conditions
  • process selected information locally
  • identify potential anomalies
  • communicate with other UAVs
  • coordinate monitoring tasks
  • adapt its trajectory to conditions
  • contribute to a shared environmental intelligence map

Host organisation

EIAS Lab is part of the International Council for Research & Innovation in Sci, Tech & Engg (ICRI-STE), which connects research with industrial translation.
